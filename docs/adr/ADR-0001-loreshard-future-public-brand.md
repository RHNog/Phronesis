# ADR-0001: LoreShard as the intended future public brand

- **Decision status:** Accepted for private strategy; public use gated
- **Decision date:** 2026-08-07
- **Feature:** `PHR-BRAND-001`
- **Brand decision ID:** `BRAND-ADR-LORESHARD-001`

## Context

Phronesis is the project’s internal codename and active build identity. The platform’s intended scope has expanded beyond card scanning into identification, market intelligence, buying, inventory, ledger, marketplace, and integration workflows across collectible commerce and potentially other physical assets. A future public identity needs a stronger collector-native commercial story and modular architecture.

The naming search considered technical, classical, mythic, navigation, measurement, and collectible-knowledge families. LoreShard best retained the central proposition—**No single price is the market**—while expressing accumulated knowledge assembled from fragments. The founder reports acquiring `loreshard.com` and `loreshard.com.br`.

Preliminary desk research returned **GO WITH CONDITIONS**. It did not produce legal clearance. Both roots are crowded or weak, “lore shard(s)” is natural fantasy/game language, and “shard” has technical and crypto meanings.

## Decision

1. **LoreShard is the intended future public brand.**
2. **Phronesis remains the internal codename and active build identity until an approved launch and migration.**
3. LoreShard remains confidential and not public.
4. Public use is blocked pending U.S. and Brazilian counsel clearance, namespace checks, identity approval, a migration plan, product readiness, and founder approval.
5. LoreShard must be used as a source-identifying compound. “Lore,” “shard,” and “shards” must not become generic product vocabulary.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Preserves a memorable metaphor for multi-source collectible intelligence.
- Supports a module family spanning Scan, Market, Buylist, Vault, Ledger, and API.
- Retains Phronesis as the philosophy and stable engineering identity during validation.
- Gives counsel, agents, designers, and product teams a single private record.

### Costs and constraints

- The dual-name period requires careful confidentiality and terminology controls.
- Legal and namespace uncertainty remains material.
- Broad enforcement of the component words is unlikely.
- A future migration requires explicit planning and may be rejected if clearance fails.

## Non-goals

This ADR does not rename source code, UI labels, applications, repositories, packages, environments, domains, deployments, or public assets. It does not authorize trademark filing, public disclosure, a landing page, marketing, DNS, SEO, or launch.

## Supersession and review

Only a recorded founder decision informed by counsel may change the candidate, authorize public use, or supersede this ADR. Counsel findings and gate decisions must be added to the [LoreShard archive](../brand/loreshard/README.md) without erasing the historical record.
