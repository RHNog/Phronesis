# Preliminary visual identity direction

This is a creative brief, not a final design system and not permission to produce public assets.

## Desired character

Dark, premium, modern, geometric, precise, intelligence-driven, collectible-native, and subtly artifact-like.

> **Bloomberg terminal meets fantasy artifact, but cleaner.**

## Concept territories

1. **Shard constellation:** fragments or nodes form a coherent signal, mark, or symbol.
2. **Fragmented codex mark:** an abstract page or tablet resolves into structured pieces.
3. **Market signal crystal:** a precise geometric signal form, never a literal magic crystal.
4. **LS monogram hidden in shards:** professional enough for an app icon and enterprise deck.

## Color territory

- Deep black or charcoal.
- Antique gold or brass.
- Muted sapphire or signal blue.
- Off-white parchment accent.

Accessibility, contrast, reproduction, dark/light environments, and screen calibration must be tested before approval.

## Avoid

- Fantasy RPG or guild-logo styling.
- Literal wizards, swords, dragons, treasure, or magic crystals.
- Neon crypto purple as the default.
- Blockchain hexagons or token imagery.
- Ornamental medieval typography.
- Visual claims of financial performance.

## Evaluation criteria

The identity must work at favicon size, in scanner and mobile contexts, on dense data screens, in print, and in enterprise presentations. It should feel at home in collectible culture without appearing to be a game item or speculative-asset scheme.
