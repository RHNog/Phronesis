Do not use direct Tolkien/Middle-earth names, including Palantir, Silmaril, Arkenstone, Mithril, Numenor, Gondor, Valinor, Eldar, Aulë, Durin, Orthanc, or Istari. Use archetypes only, and require counsel review for any later fantasy-derived candidate.
