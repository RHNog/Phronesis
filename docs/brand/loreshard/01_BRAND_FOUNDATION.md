## Objective

Establish a durable internal foundation for LoreShard while preserving Phronesis as the active engineering and build identity until a separate, counsel-informed launch and migration decision.

## Why A Successor Brand Was Needed

Phronesis is philosophically strong but operationally difficult as a broad customer-facing name. It is unfamiliar to many audiences, has pronunciation and spelling friction, and reads more like an internal philosophy than an immediate collectible-commerce proposition.

The platform also outgrew any name that framed it as only a scanner, price checker, or trading-card utility. The intended system joins physical-asset recognition, multi-provider market evidence, dealer knowledge, buying operations, inventory, accounting evidence, commerce automation, and APIs. A public brand needs room for this broader role.

The naming effort therefore sought a brand that could:

- carry the practical-wisdom philosophy of Phronesis without requiring a philosophy lesson;
- feel native to collectors without becoming childish or game-specific;
- communicate fragmented evidence becoming usable intelligence;
- support professional dealers, marketplaces, collectors, and developers;
- extend from cards into other valuable physical assets;
- work as a master brand with understandable modules;
- remain memorable, pronounceable, and commercially useful in the U.S. and Brazil;
- avoid obvious conflicts before emotional and financial commitment.

## Business Definition

LoreShard is intended to name **an intelligence and operating layer for collectible commerce and alternative physical assets**.

### Expected Capabilities

- AI/computer-vision identification of cards and other collectibles.
- Scanner-controlled intake workflows.
- Card, set, variant, language, foil, edition, and printing recognition.
- Sealed-product recognition.
- Price aggregation and marketplace comparison.
- Recent-sales and comparable-sales intelligence.
- Liquidity, sales velocity, spread, and inventory-depth analysis.
- Buying recommendations and offer calculation.
- Vendor-buying, bulk-intake, and event workflows.
- Inventory and collection management.
- Valuation, provenance, cost basis, margin, and sales analysis.
- Marketplace listing automation, repricing, and alerts.
- APIs, developer tools, internal automation, and integrations.

## Core Idea

> **Markets are made of fragments. LoreShard makes them intelligible.**

Collectible markets do not have one complete source of truth. Identity, condition, language, treatment, venue, time, liquidity, inventory, shipping, fees, transaction history, and dealer knowledge all alter the meaning of a price. Each provider offers only a partial view.

LoreShard’s role is to assemble, weight, explain, and operationalize those fragments without pretending that one number is the whole market.

## Meaning Of Lore

Within this brand, **Lore** means:

- accumulated knowledge;
- market memory;
- hard-won expertise;
- hidden context;
- collector knowledge;
- provenance;
- pattern recognition;
- what experienced dealers “just know”;
- the context behind objects, markets, and value.

Lore is not mystical certainty. It is accumulated context interpreted with discipline. That preserves the spirit of **Phronesis: practical wisdom applied to real decisions**.

## Meaning Of Shard

Within this brand, **Shard** means:

- a fragment of a larger truth;
- one piece of market evidence;
- one signal among many;
- a relic-like piece of knowledge;
- something incomplete until interpreted.

“Shard” is metaphorical brand meaning, not the generic name of a data object. Product vocabulary must continue to use market signals, source observations, market evidence, and comparable observations.

## Combined Meaning

- **LoreShard = fragmented knowledge made useful.**
- **LoreShard = scattered market evidence assembled into practical intelligence.**

## Continuity With Phronesis

Phronesis supplies the internal philosophy:

- evidence before conclusions;
- observations separated from reasoning;
- explainable intelligence;
- contextual judgment;
- human decision ownership;
- practical action over opaque scoring.

LoreShard translates that philosophy into a more memorable public metaphor. It does not retire or replace Phronesis inside the build until the migration gate is explicitly approved.

## Brand Principles

1. **Practical intelligence over spectacle.** Every claim should help a user decide or act.
2. **Evidence over certainty theater.** Source quality and limitations remain visible.
3. **Collector-native, not collector-exclusive.** The brand understands the culture but can serve serious commerce.
4. **Calm confidence over hype.** Trust should come from rigor, not exaggerated promises.
5. **One market view from many signals.** No single price, provider, or observation is the market.
6. **Human judgment remains sovereign.** LoreShard informs decisions; it does not erase operator responsibility.
7. **Expandable without becoming vague.** The brand can extend to physical alternative assets while retaining collectible-commerce credibility.

## Success Conditions

- Future collaborators can state the Phronesis/LoreShard boundary without ambiguity.
- Counsel receives a precise, non-inflated goods/services and risk brief.
- Designers can explore a coherent visual territory without producing fantasy-RPG or crypto clichés.
- Product leads can name modules without genericizing the mark.
- No public action occurs before the launch gates are satisfied.

## Non-Goals

- Legal clearance or legal advice.
- A logo or final visual identity.
- Public messaging, SEO, domain activation, or launch collateral.
- Code, repository, package, UI, or infrastructure renaming.
- A claim that every future category is currently supported.
