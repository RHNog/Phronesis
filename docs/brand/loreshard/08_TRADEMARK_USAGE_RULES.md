# Trademark and usage rules

> **Own LoreShard.**
> **Do not try to own lore.**
> **Do not try to own shard.**
> **Do not make “shards” the generic units of the product.**

These are preventive brand rules, not a statement that any trademark rights or registration already exist. Counsel must approve final trademark form, symbols, notices, disclaimers, and filing claims.

## Source-identifying use

Use LoreShard as the name identifying the source of software and intelligence:

- LoreShard platform
- LoreShard software
- LoreShard intelligence
- LoreShard API
- LoreShard Scan
- LoreShard Market

## Generic vocabulary

Use:

- market signals;
- source observations;
- pricing signals;
- sales signals;
- inventory signals;
- market evidence;
- comparable observations;
- collectible signals.

Do not call ordinary data inputs:

- lore shards;
- market shards;
- data shards;
- evidence shards;
- shards.

**Correct:** LoreShard analyzes market signals.
**Incorrect:** LoreShard analyzes lore shards.

## Grammar and naming rules

- Use **LoreShard** as one word with capital L and S.
- Do not pluralize the brand as “LoreShards,” except when discussing a defensive domain candidate.
- Do not use LoreShard as a verb.
- Do not write “a loreshard.”
- Do not describe inputs as “loreshards.”
- Do not normalize “shard” as a generic noun in product copy, schemas, APIs, databases, code, or support language.

## Review trigger

Every public naming, UI, API, schema, campaign, partner agreement, and support guide must pass a usage review before LoreShard launch. Suspected misuse should be corrected early and recorded.
