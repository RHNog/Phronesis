## Business Being Named

The platform is not merely a card scanner. It is intended to become **an intelligence and operating layer for collectible commerce and alternative physical assets**.

Its expected scope spans identification, scanner-controlled intake, exact collectible identity, sealed-product recognition, multi-source market evidence, recent and comparable sales, liquidity and inventory-depth analysis, recommendations and offer calculation, vendor workflows, inventory and collection management, valuation, cost basis, margin and sales analytics, listing and repricing automation, alerts, APIs, and integrations.

Initial categories include Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece, Lorcana, Riftbound, sports cards, and other trading cards. Potential expansion includes comics, coins, memorabilia, toys, sealed collectibles, sneakers, watches, luxury collectibles, and other valuable physical assets.

## Core Brand Thesis

> **Markets are made of fragments. LoreShard makes them intelligible.**

- **Primary positioning:** LoreShard — Practical intelligence for collectible commerce.
- **Strategic proposition:** No single price is the market.
- **Explainer:** LoreShard turns scattered market evidence into collectible-commerce intelligence.
- **Practical promise:** Know what you’re looking at. Know what it’s worth. Know what to do next.
- **Operational pitch:** Identify inventory. Understand value. Act with confidence.

Founder-level thesis:

> LoreShard is the practical intelligence platform for collectible commerce, built on the belief that no single price is the market. It turns scattered signals from marketplaces, inventory, transactions, and dealer knowledge into clearer decisions.

## Why The Name Works

**Lore** represents accumulated knowledge, market memory, expertise, provenance, hidden context, pattern recognition, and what experienced dealers “just know.” It preserves the Phronesis spirit of practical wisdom applied to real decisions.

**Shard** represents one fragment of a larger truth: one source observation, signal, sale, inventory fact, or piece of evidence that remains incomplete until interpreted.

Combined:

- **LoreShard = fragmented knowledge made useful.**
- **LoreShard = scattered market evidence assembled into practical intelligence.**

## Non-Negotiable Doctrine

> **Own LoreShard. Do not try to own lore. Do not try to own shard. Do not make “shards” the generic units of the product.**

Use generic terms such as **market signals**, **source observations**, **pricing signals**, **sales signals**, **inventory signals**, **market evidence**, **comparable observations**, and **collectible signals**.

Correct: `LoreShard analyzes market signals.`
Incorrect: `LoreShard analyzes lore shards.`

## Principal Risks

- Natural fantasy/game use of “lore shard(s)” creates collision and distinctiveness risk.
- `LORE` and `SHARD` are individually crowded and weak roots.
- “Shard” has established database, distributed-systems, blockchain, and crypto meanings.
- App-store, common-law, package, social, and international conflicts have not been fully verified.
- Broad enforcement may be difficult even if the compound is registrable.
- Public use before counsel review could create avoidable opposition, filing, and migration costs.
