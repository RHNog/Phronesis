# Voice and messaging

## Archetype

**The Sage-Merchant:** experienced, evidence-led, commercially practical, and calm.

LoreShard should feel intelligent, collector-native, rigorous, precise, slightly mythic, and serious enough for dealers and marketplaces. It should not feel childish, like wizard cosplay, crypto-bro marketing, or corporate beige.

It is not the wizard, banker, gamer, spreadsheet robot, or crypto prophet.

## Voice principles

- Explain evidence and uncertainty plainly.
- Prefer a useful decision over a grand claim.
- Respect dealer knowledge and category nuance.
- Use concise, active language.
- Let the fantasy layer remain implied rather than screamed.

## Preferred vocabulary

Practical intelligence, market signals, collectible commerce, confidence, evidence, value, inventory, liquidity, decision, signal quality, source weighting, market memory, dealer-grade intelligence, comparable observations, and source freshness.

## Avoid or tightly control

Mystical prophecy, magic crystals, “unlock the secrets,” ancient wisdom, shards of destiny, Web3, investment alpha, financial freedom, moonshots, guaranteed value, and language implying certainty without evidence.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Position:** Practical intelligence for collectible commerce.
2. **Problem:** No single price is the market.
3. **Method:** Reconcile scattered market, inventory, transaction, and dealer signals.
4. **Outcome:** Know what it is, what it is worth, and what to do next.
5. **Proof:** Exact identity, source traceability, freshness, confidence, liquidity, and workflow action.

## Example copy

**Preferred:** “Compare current market signals, understand their confidence, and move from identification to an informed offer.”

**Avoid:** “Unlock ancient shards of market wisdom and discover your next moonshot.”

All public messaging remains draft until legal, brand, and launch review.
