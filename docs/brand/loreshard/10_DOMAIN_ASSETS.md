## Stewardship checklist

- Verify registrar, registrant entity, administrative contacts, MFA, recovery methods, transfer lock, auto-renewal, payment continuity, and expiration dates.
- Record access in an approved secret-management system; never place credentials in this repository.
- Monitor DNS, certificate-transparency, email, redirect, and WHOIS exposure for unintended disclosure.
- Keep domains parked privately or inert until the launch strategy is approved.
- Establish renewal alerts independent of the registrar.

## Pending defensive evaluation

Gate 2 should evaluate—not automatically acquire—`.ai`, common country variants, plural forms such as `loreshards`, typo domains, and relevant social/developer namespaces. The cost, defensive benefit, disclosure risk, and counsel advice should determine action.

## Prohibited actions under current state

Do not publish content, redirect to Phronesis, configure public analytics, create indexed SEO pages, advertise the domains, or use them for an external email campaign.
