# Trademark counsel brief

## Background

The project is currently built under the internal codename **Phronesis**. The founder intends **LoreShard** to become the future public master brand, but no public launch or technical rename is authorized. The founder reports acquiring `loreshard.com` and `loreshard.com.br`.

The platform is intended to provide software and data intelligence for collectible commerce and alternative physical assets: computer-vision identification, scanner intake, exact-printing recognition, pricing and comparable-sales intelligence, liquidity analysis, offer calculation, vendor workflows, inventory and collection management, valuation, cost basis, margin and sales analytics, marketplace automation, alerts, APIs, and integrations.

Initial categories include Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece, Lorcana, Riftbound, sports cards, and other trading cards. Potential future categories include comics, coins, memorabilia, toys, sealed collectibles, sneakers, watches, luxury collectibles, and other valuable physical assets.

## Requested jurisdictions and classes

- United States.
- Brazil.
- Likely Nice Classes **9, 35, 36, and 42**, with counsel requested to advise whether Class 36 should be included, narrowed, deferred, or avoided.

## Preliminary risk context

Supplied desk research reached **GO WITH CONDITIONS**, not legal clearance. It assessed U.S. and Brazil risk as moderate, fantasy/gaming collision risk as high because “lore shard(s)” can be natural game vocabulary, and crypto/database confusion as moderate because “shard” is a common technical term. Both roots appear crowded or weak independently.
