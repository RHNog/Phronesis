# PHR-ARCH-017 — Personal Market Providers And Cost Structure

- Approved permanent users now have `My settings` separate from Administration.
- TCGplayer, LigaMagic, LigaPokémon, and PriceCharting are included and enabled by default; users choose which evidence appears in their Vendor Workspace.
- Users can override cross-border costs and decision targets while leaving any field on the current workspace default.
- Official exchange-rate evidence, provider credentials, module assignment, and owner policy remain outside personal control.
- Personal settings work through restricted-public permanent login without exposing Administration.
